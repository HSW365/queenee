FAQ

Straight answers

I don't have a website at all. Can I still sign up?

Yes. Choose "I need a new website" when you sign up. We build it from the business details you give us.

How long does it take?

We confirm your build timeline by email right after you sign up, based on the size of your site.

How does the CallTwin payment work?

$99 is charged today with your website. Then $99 is charged once a month for the next 5 months. After the final payment the plan ends on its own — nothing to cancel.

Can I add CallTwin later?

Yes, but adding it at sign-up means it is trained on your business during the build and connected to your site's call button at launch.

Do I own my website?

Yes. The site and its content are yours. We help connect it to a domain you own.

What if a monthly CallTwin payment fails?

Stripe retries the card automatically. CallTwin pauses if the payment stays unpaid and resumes once it is paid.
